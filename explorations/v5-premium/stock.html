Our verdict

Accumulate

A position to build with patience. The market is paying for the story it can see; the value sits in the margin it cannot yet measure.

Conviction High | Horizon 3–5 years | Last read Today

The reasoning

Cloud is no longer the growth story; it is the engine. Azure compounds above thirty percent while its operating margin quietly widens, and that combination is rare enough that the broader multiple still understates it.

The artificial intelligence build-out, once a drag on free cash flow, has turned self-funding. Capital spending is heavy, yet the workloads filling that capacity now arrive with pricing power attached. Few companies of this scale grow and earn at once.

The single caution is expectation. Much is already priced, and a soft quarter would test patient hands. We treat that not as a reason to wait, but as a reason to build slowly. The thesis is sound; the discipline is in the pacing.
