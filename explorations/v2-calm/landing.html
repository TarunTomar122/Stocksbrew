The one thing

The rate cut everyone wanted is already in the price

The market spent six weeks pricing in a cut that the Fed all but confirmed yesterday. So when it arrived, stocks barely moved. That is the point worth sitting with: the news was true and also a non-event, because the crowd had already done the math.

For a long-term holder, none of this changes the case for anything you own. It is a good week to do nothing, on purpose.
