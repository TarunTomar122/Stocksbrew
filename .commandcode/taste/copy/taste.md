# copy
- Never use em dashes in copy — they read like AI slop. Confidence: 0.95
- Never truncate or cut off text with ellipsis — always show full text. Confidence: 0.90
- Keep copy short, outcome-based, and human-sounding; outcome-based means free users should feel they need to buy, and pro users should feel their subscription is worth continuing. Confidence: 0.9
- Remove left borders from cards and components when asked. Confidence: 0.80
- Structure marketing pages per "Sell Like Crazy" (Sabri Suby): hero = call out + big promise, one idea per section, buttons say what happens, one CTA repeated, sentence case headings. Confidence: 0.8
- Apply the "give away the answer, charge for the application" principle: give away information (prices, news, earnings) freely, but charge for the application of that information (AI agent calls, unlimited stocks, advanced alerts, rebalancing). Confidence: 0.9
