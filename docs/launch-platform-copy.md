## Description · ~500 words

You do not need more tickers. You need a clear answer on the ones you already follow.

**stocksbrew** is stock research built around **your list**. Not a generic market feed. Not another chart terminal. A place that reads the messy inputs for each name you care about and returns a **personalized call**: buy, hold, sell, or watch.

### What changes when you use it

**On Radar, every holding and watchlist stock gets a stance you can act on.** Instead of opening news, Reddit, filings, and price charts separately, you see one research-backed read: what changed, why it matters, what to do, what to watch, and what to avoid. Outcome: you spend minutes, not hours, getting oriented before a trade, a trim, or a hold-through-earnings call.

That matters because most mistakes are not missing data. They are acting late, acting without a frame, or holding simply because the next step was unclear.

**Triggers and email alerts turn intent into timing.** Set levels or zones on Radar names. When price or setup crosses into a **buy or sell window**, stocksbrew emails you. Outcome: you are less likely to miss the move you were already watching for, and less likely to stare at screens all day “just in case.”

**Per-stock pages connect the call to the evidence.** Scorecards, fundamentals, technicals, catalysts, analyst context, and AI synthesis on one ticker. Outcome: when you disagree with a call, you can see *why* in plain language, not a black-box score.

**Discovery tools widen the funnel without drowning you.** **Market Heat** surfaces unusual attention and setups. **Market Anomalies** flags moves worth a second look. **Screens** bundle growth, quality, momentum, and value lists. Outcome: you find names worth researching, add them to Radar, and get the same call-plus-alert workflow you rely on for core holdings.

**Earnings Intel gives you the “so what” after the print.** Free post-earnings reports: what happened, what it means, what to do next. Outcome: fewer panicked reactions and fewer holds-by-default because the call was unclear.

**Portfolio Analyzer and Should I Buy?** answer book-level and one-off questions. Paste holdings for concentration and theme reads, or run a fast scorecard on any ticker. Outcome: portfolio decisions and “should I even own this?” moments get the same clarity as your core watchlist.

**Risk flags show up on your names** (RSI stretch, 52-week pressure, earnings windows) so you can react while the thesis still makes sense.

**Discovery feeds action:** Heat or Screens → open a ticker → read the call → add to Radar → set a trigger → get emailed in zone.

**Free:** 3 Radar stocks, no credit card. **Pro:** unlimited Radar, full calls, alerts. Start on real tickers you already care about, then expand when the book grows.

Built for **US stocks** today. Research and education only. We are not a broker and not investment advice.

## Target audience

**Primary**

- Individual investors in **US equities** who actively manage a portfolio or watchlist (roughly 5–40 names).
- People who already read news, check earnings, or lurk investing Reddit but still feel unsure about **buy vs hold vs sell**.
- Side-hustle and full-time retail investors who want **action clarity**, not more dashboards.

**Secondary**

- Newer investors overwhelmed by Finviz, Yahoo, and X feeds who want **scoped guidance on their own tickers**.
- Investors post-earnings who need a fast **“so what”** read (Earnings Intel, stock calls).
- Users comparing screeners who want a **post-discovery workflow** (Screens/Heat → Radar → alerts).

**Not for (today)**

- Day traders needing sub-second execution tooling.
- Crypto, options-only, or non-US markets (US stocks focus for now).
- Users who want a brokerage or trade execution inside the product.

## Problem it solves

1. **Too much data, too little direction.** Feeds tell you *what happened*. They rarely tell you *what to do* with *your* shares.
2. **Research is fragmented.** Fundamentals live in one tab, news in another, Reddit somewhere else, earnings in a PDF. You rebuild the story manually every time.
3. **Timing slips.** You meant to watch a level or a post-earnings reaction. Life happened. The stock moved without you.
4. **Generic tools ignore your book.** Screeners and heat maps are market-wide. Your holdings need context: trim, hold, add, or walk away.
5. **Decision fatigue.** “Should I sell?” loops burn time and emotion when there is no plain stance backed by evidence.

---

## Our solution

**stocksbrew** scopes everything to **your Radar**: holdings plus watchlist.

For each stock, we synthesize **fundamentals, news, Reddit, earnings, and price/technicals** into a **personalized buy, hold, sell, or watch call**, with explicit **what to watch** and **what to avoid**.

You **set triggers** on Radar names and receive **emails** when setups hit **buy or sell zones** or when risk signals matter.

Around that core loop we add:

- **Stock pages** with scorecards and catalyst context
- **Market Heat** and **Anomalies** for discovery
- **Screens** for themed lists
- **Earnings Intel** for post-earnings guidance (free)
- **Should I Buy?** and **Portfolio Analyzer** for one-off and book-level questions

Start free on **3 Radar stocks**. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited tracking, full calls, and alerts.
