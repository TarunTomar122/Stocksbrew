## Product truth (north star)

What stocksbrew actually is:

1. **Personalized recommendations** on what to do with *your* stocks (buy, hold, sell, watch), not generic market commentary.
2. **Research engine** reads fundamentals, news, Reddit conversations, and earnings (plus price/technicals) and turns that into a clear call.
3. **Radar** is where you follow favorites (holdings + watchlist).
4. **Triggers & alerts** let you set levels or zones; you get **automatic emails** when something moves into a buy or sell zone (or similar actionable shift).

**We are not selling:** a newsletter cadence, "morning read", or "twice a day" rhythm. The value is *action clarity* and *timely alerts*, not schedule.

## Voice rules (global)

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "What to do", "buy / hold / sell", "your stocks" | "Twice a day", "twice-daily", "morning and evening", "digest schedule" |
| Name the inputs when helpful: fundamentals, news, Reddit, earnings | Vague "AI-powered insights" with no substance |
| Triggers, alerts, buy zone, sell zone | "Directives", "institutional-grade intelligence" |
| Short sentences. Plain English. | Em dashes `—` |

**Punctuation:** No `—`. Use `.` or `,`.

**Brand name:** `stocksbrew` (lowercase wordmark).

**Domain:** `https://www.stocksbrew.online`

### Words to avoid (sitewide)

| Avoid | Use instead |
| --- | --- |
| twice a day, twice daily, twice-daily | personalized calls, recommendations, guidance, alerts |
| morning / evening briefs | when it matters, when a zone hits, on your stocks |
| daily editor, digest rhythm | clear call, trigger fired, email alert |
