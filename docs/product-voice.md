# stocksbrew product voice

This is the current source of truth for user-facing copy. It replaces the positioning in `docs/brand-copy.md` where the two disagree.

## The one-line answer

stocksbrew helps people analyse and track US stocks.

## Approved homepage copy

**Headline:** Analyse and track US stocks with AI

**Supporting line:** Search any US stock for financials, news and market context.

The search field is the primary action. Radar is the next step after someone finds a stock worth following.

## Product hierarchy

1. **Analyse:** open any US stock for financials, news, price history and market context.
2. **Track:** add holdings or watchlist names to Radar so their research stays current.
3. **Return:** use alerts for earnings, price levels and important changes.
4. **Explore:** Pulse, Daily and Social provide wider market context. They support the core product; they are not the homepage promise.

## How we write

- Start with what happened or what the person can do.
- Prefer ordinary verbs: search, read, track, compare, explain, alert.
- Name the object. Say “news about Apple” instead of “relevant intelligence.”
- Keep interface copy to one idea per sentence.
- Use numbers when they clarify a financial fact. Do not turn paragraphs into scoreboards.
- State uncertainty plainly: “We have not found a confirmed reason for the move.”
- Keep source names and timestamps close to financial claims.
- Write for a person who follows stocks but does not work at a trading desk.

## What we avoid

- AI-powered insights
- actionable intelligence
- institutional-grade
- stay ahead of the market
- cut through the noise
- the tape, calm tape, green board, modest bid
- repeated “this, not that” or “not just X, but Y” constructions
- claims that the product predicts returns or guarantees a better decision
- fake friendliness, forced slang, rhetorical hype and filler introductions

Internal data fields may retain technical names such as `tape` or `signal`. They must be translated before they reach a person.

## Before and after

| Avoid | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Initiate AI-powered research | Start researching this stock |
| Source-backed movers | Movers with a confirmed reason |
| Current stock intelligence | Latest research |
| Crowd signal | What investors are saying |
| The setup changed | Something important changed |
| AI brief pending | We’re researching this stock |
| No actionable signals | Nothing needs your attention right now |

## Generated writing

Use one strong generation call with the evidence attached. Prompts should require a concrete lead, short paragraphs, source-backed facts, and an honest missing-evidence state. Do not add a second model as a reviewer and do not score prose mechanically.

## Editorial publishing rule

A blog URL is indexable only after a deliberate editorial review. Review means the page has a clear search intent, useful original structure or product data, current primary or authoritative sources, honest limitations, accurate metadata, and a reason to exist beside the other published pages. New posts default to unpublished from search until they are added to the reviewed set.
