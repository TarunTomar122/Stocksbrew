What stocksbrew actually is

“Know what to do with your stocks.” A US stock research app that turns a watchlist into plain-English calls — Buy / Hold / Sell / Watch — by fusing fundamentals, news, Reddit, earnings, and price action. You add names to Radar, set trigger zones, and get emailed when something moves into a buy or sell zone. Plus: today's big movers (Anomalies), crowd sentiment (Reddit Buzz), ranked setups (Market Heat), pre-market gaps, and per-ticker AI briefs. Free for 3 stocks → Pro.

The category & where we win

Who's adjacent

  • Seeking Alpha — crowd essays + quant ratings. Breadth, but you still decide.
  • Motley Fool — newsletter stock picks. Passive; it's their call, not yours.
  • Danelfin — AI score 1–10 to beat the market. A number, not a stance.
  • Simply Wall St — “snowflake” visual scores. Pretty, judgment left to you.
  • TipRanks — analyst & insider ranks. Wisdom of the street.
  • Finchat / Magnifi — chat-with-data. Tool, not a call.

Our wedge (the converting idea)

  • ⚡ They sell data and scores. We sell the decision.
  • ⚡ Most tools are pull (you must remember to look). We are push — email when a name hits your zone.
  • ⚡ We're personal to your holdings, not a general picks feed.
  • ⚡ One readable call beats five dashboards. The copy is the product.

So the winning landing doesn't say "powerful AI." It shows one real verdict on a ticker you recognize and a button that says "get this for your 3 free stocks."

What converts most for this category (and what each direction leans on)

1. Show the product working on a known name. A real call on NVDA / TSLA beats any feature grid. Proof > promise.

2. Lead with the free offer + low commitment. “Free for 3 stocks, no card.” Friction is the #1 killer in finance.

3. Make the push feel real. Alerts/emails as a hero metaphor — the thing competitors underdeliver on.

4. Contrast chaos vs. our calm. The pain (5 tabs, Reddit panic, Finviz overload) → our one read.

5. Personalize instantly. “Enter your ticker → see your call.” Zero-to-value in one input.

Z01–Z04 attack the top of this list directly (highest expected conversion). Z05–Z10 explore secondary / personality-led angles.
